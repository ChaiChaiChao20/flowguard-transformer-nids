    Attention architecture

    • Feature encoder: value tokens, feature identities and CLS
    • Temporal encoder: chronological flows and a causal mask
    • TabTransformer: contextual protocol/state categories
    • Flow-grid ViT: numeric patches with learned positions
    • BERT-style means scratch-trained bidirectional feature attention

    Federated and online extensions

    • FedAvg: three local simulated clients, three fixed rounds
    • Client updates weighted by training sample count
    • Online stream: predict, reveal true labels, then update
    • Page-Hinkley monitors increases in observed chunk loss
    • No distributed privacy or unlabeled adaptation claim
